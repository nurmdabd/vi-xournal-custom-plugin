* Hand: `space`
* Selection: `k`
* Pen: `w`
* Highlighter: `h`
* Color mode: `c`
* Tex: `i`
* Delete: `x`
* Eraser: `e`
* Redo: `y`
* Tool mode: `t`
* Text: `<Shift>t`, `<Shift>i`
* Undo: `d`, `u`, `z`
